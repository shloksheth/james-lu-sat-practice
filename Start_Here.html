Your SAT practice library

199 original lesson questions + 112 selected bank questions. Daily PDFs include answer explanations. Cumulative PDFs are linked test booklets: open each bank question online, record the answer, then review its explanation after the test. Active questions were excluded during selection.

How to use it

  1. Finish a lesson, then complete its practice without the answer key. The Desmos and Grammar files follow the course's numbered days. Reading and Non-Desmos have standalone lesson files because those courses do not assign numbered days.
  2. For Desmos, try the method from the lesson, then verify with substitution or algebra. Keep all constraints and the exact requested quantity in view.
  3. After a section, take its cumulative test. Review wrong answers and correct guesses. Return to the relevant daily practice rather than repeating only the exact missed question.

Your review priorities

Based on the recorded PSAT errors, start with Information and Ideas: exact-claim evidence, quantitative evidence, and inference. Next review Craft and Structure: words in context and text purpose. Review Standard English Conventions: clause boundaries and paired dashes. In math, emphasize all-equation systems, combined inequalities, quadratic coefficients after removing outliers, completing the square, and products of roots.

A focused question-bank cycle: 8 Command of Evidence, 5 Inferences, 2 Central Ideas and Details, 2 Words in Context, 2 Text Structure and Purpose, 1 Cross-Text Connections, and 5 Transitions = the included 25-question reading test. For Khan Academy's Digital SAT Reading and Writing, prioritize those same skill names; use the included James Lu reading, punctuation, verb, and function videos as the video review. Khan Academy: what to do first

Open Khan Academy SAT Reading and Writing. Start at Medium because your practice-test accuracy suggests targeted review rather than restarting every foundation lesson. These counts are a recommended first review cycle, not extra worksheets in this library.

PriorityUnit and skillPractice target
1Unit 5, Medium: Information and ideas - Command of Evidence (textual), Command of Evidence (quantitative), Inferences10 of each = 30
2Unit 6, Medium: Craft and structure - Words in Context; Text Structure and Purpose8 of each = 16
3Unit 7, Medium: Expression of ideas + standard English conventions - Boundaries10

After at least 8 correct out of the last 10 in a skill without guessing, try its Advanced counterpart: Information and ideas in Unit 8, Craft and structure in Unit 9, or Expression of ideas + standard English conventions in Unit 10. Do 10 Advanced questions in each remaining weak skill. Use a Foundations lesson only when you cannot explain the rule or reasoning after review.

Videos to revisit for your errors

  • James Lu: How to Solve Reading Comprehension Questions - exact claims, evidence, qualifiers, and eliminating unsupported details.
  • James Lu: Improve Your Reading Score Overnight - inference reasoning and pacing. Treat timing advice as a strategy to test, not a score guarantee.
  • James Lu: Never Miss Another SAT Transition Question - contrast, cause, example, and clarification relationships.
  • James Lu: Punctuation, First Half - clause boundaries and paired punctuation.
  • James Lu: Verb Questions - subject heads, finite predicates, and verb forms.

Scope and sources

Includes all 24 instructional videos across the four core courses mapped in the signed-in classroom: Desmos Mastery, Grammar Mastery, Non-Desmos, and Reading Comprehension. Diagnostic materials and agenda-only pages are excluded. Lessons were reviewed through full transcripts and selected worked-example video frames, including graphs and equations; this was not continuous real-time playback of every video.

Original practice is independently authored and is not endorsed by College Board or James Lu. Bank items are linked from SATQuestionBank.org. Preserve your Bluebook practice tests by retaining the active-question exclusion when choosing more bank practice.
